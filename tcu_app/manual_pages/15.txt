The actual fine temperature controlling of the water to within
± 0.02°C of the set temperature cannot be activated until this
stage has been reached.
This fine temperature control procedure is initiated by the
command START. After this command has been entered
and acknowledged, the internal and external pumps are activated and both their respective lights on the STATUS display
panel light up. The cooling compressor switches on and this
is also correspondingly signalled by the constant illumination
of the COMPR light. The heater light blinks in time with the
rapid switching on and off of the heater which is characteristic for the fine-tuning mode of the temperature control procedure.
The Temperature Control Unit and connected system is now
filled with water and temperature controlled accurately to
within ± 0.02°C of the desired set temperature.
