ASM Operating Software

* ASM unit:
– Dip switch setting next to V24 interface
ON = switch up
OFF = switch down
1 – ON without parity
2 – ON even
3 – OFF with RTS/CTS
4 – OFF
5 – OFF
6 – ON 2400 Baud
7 – ON Computer operation
– Switching on unit
The dip switch setting is only effective when
switching on. Alterations made during operation
are ignored.
