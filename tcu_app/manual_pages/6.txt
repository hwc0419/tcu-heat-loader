3. Packaging List
For reasons of packaging convenience, the ASM unit is split
into the following subassemblies.
a) Temperature Control Unit – Controller (hereafter
referred to as TCU – Controller)
b) Temperature Control Unit (hereafter referred to as
TCU)
All other accessories (e.g. hoses, adaptors, fittings, front
plates etc.) are supplied in the same packaging casing as the
TCU except connection cables A and B which are supplied
with the TCU – Controller.
