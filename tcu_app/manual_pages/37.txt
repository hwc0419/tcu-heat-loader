8. Refrigeration Circuit
(see 12.1 Refrigeration circuit diagram)
8.1 Components
1 Compressor:
(reciprocating compressor, hermetic type). The motorwinding is cooled by the returning refrigerant gas from
the evaporator. The compressor holds the oil charge
for lubrication. The oil circulates along the entire refrigeration circuit. (See 8.2 Oil cycle for further details).
2 Discharge line:
(insulated copper tube). Transports superheated compressed gas from the compressor to the condenser.
The connection to the discharge hose and the high
pressure hose is cut off.
3 Condenser:
(welded stainless steel plate heat exchanger). Hot superheated compressed refrigeration gas from the compressor is condensed in the condenser due to heat exchange. The heat load (superheat and condensation
heat) is removed by the cooling water system (inlet
temperature min. 15°C, max. 30°C) at a refrigerant
temperature (condensation temperature) of 30°C. The
saturation pressure of the refrigerant R134a at 30°C is
7.7 bar abs. This pressure (condensation pressure) is
measured at the discharge hose. Condensation temperature and pressure rise up to a maximum of 35°C
at 14 bar abs as the cooling water inlet temperature
reaches it’s maximum temperature of 30°C and the
cooling water inlet pressure drops to it’s minimum pressure of 0.5 bar.
The cooling water outlet temperature and the cooling
water flow also vary as a function of the cooling water
inlet temperature and pressure. The cooling water outlet temperature ranges between 35°C – 40°C and cooling water flow between 2 – 12 l/min.
4 Cooling water economizer:
(condensation pressure control valve). This adjusts the
cooling water flow as a function of different inlet temperatures and pressures to obtain constant condensation pressure. The valve closes when the refrigeration
circuit is switched off. The preset value is 7.7 bar abs
and the indication mark is located between 3 and 4.
