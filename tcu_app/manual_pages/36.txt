7. Safety Elements
The Temperature Control Unit is equipped with a range of
safety features as a protection against faulty handling, operation or operating conditions. These are listed below.
Excess temperature protection 27.5°C ±0.5K
Low temperature protection 16.5°C ±0.5K
Low liquid level protection
Pump motor protection switch
Heating monitoring
Fault monitoring
No cooling water supply (high pressure cut-off compressor)
These features trigger off fault messages which are displayed via the 19” control module and the RS232 interface
on the computer monitor.
The overflow nozzle and solenoid valves (which are only activated when the pump is running) serve a protective function
in case of water overflowing from the temperature control circuit.
Topping up which is also displayed via the 19” control module
and the RS232 interface on the computer monitor, is required for water losses of approx. 2 to 3 liters.
