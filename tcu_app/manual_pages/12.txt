Connect the TCU Controller to the Temperature Control Unit
via the flat band connection cable A. This should be plugged
into the sockets marked A (both 37–pole Sub D sockets) of
the TCU Controller and Temperature Control Unit.
The 24 V~ mains supply for the TCU Controller is provided
by similarly connecting the 4–pole Hirschmann socket on the
TCU Controller with the 4–pole Hirschmann bushing (both
marked B) on the Temperature Control Unit using connection cable B.
The TCU Controller is connected to the RS 232 C interface
on the computer via a flat band cable which is inserted into
the 25–pole Sub D socket (marked with RS 232 C) on the
TCU Controller.
