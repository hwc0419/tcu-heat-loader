– ”ER” command (release)
The hardware safety circuit recognizes faults within the
system displays them via the red alarm LED (blinks).
Examples of faults are listed below:
– Exceeding the temperature limits 17°C / 27°C
– Sensor breakage / short circuit
– Water below ”min” level
– Pressostat (operating pressure exceeded)
– Pump 1 and pump 2 (operating temperature exceeded)
After the fault has been eliminated, the software must
also be ”released”. If the cause of the fault has not been
completely eliminated, the alarm LED carries on blinking.
The ”START” command cannot be entered as long as
the LED blinks.
– ”BS” and ”BSB”
”BS<CR>” displays the operating status in the form of
6 hexadecimal characters. These 6 characters result in
6 * 4 bit positions. Every bit characterizes one operating status. The meaning can be gathered from an additional list.
”BSB<CR>” gives the same information as ”BS<CR>”.
The 6 characters are already coded as a bit sequence.
Example:
a) ”BS<CR>” – – –> 536C64$<CR><LF>
with B1 = 53H
with B2 = 6CH
with B3 = 64H
b) ”BSB<CR>”
B1 B2 B3
– –> 1––1––11 –11–11–– –11––1––$<CR><LF>
Operating status in normal operation:
”BS<CR>” : 400000$<CR><LF>
or
”BSB<CR>”:–1–––– ––––– –––––$<CR><LF>
 ”–” = filling mark ”FULL” exceeded (no fault)
