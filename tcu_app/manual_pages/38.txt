5 Receiver bottle:
This contains the condensed refrigerant and enables
the separation of liquid and gas refrigerant.
6 Liquid line:
(copper tube). This transports the condensed refrigerant from the receiver bottle to the automatic expansion
valve.
7 Sight glass / moisture indicator:
This allows the observation of the refrigerant flow in the
liquid line and thus can indicate if there is a lack of refrigerant. If the glass is clear there is no loss of refrigerant, if there are bubbles a small leakage may be the
cause and the presence of foam indicates a large leakage. This indicator also enables the detection of humidity within the refrigeration circuit.
8 Filter / dryer:
Molecular sieves of hygroscopic material filter and dry
the liquid refrigerant continuously.
9 Automatic expansion valve:
(insulated evaporation pressure control valve). This is
a control device that throttles the refrigerant flow, (allowing the refrigerant to evaporate at a low pressure
within the evaporator), in a manner to obtain a constant
evaporation pressure and temperature. The preset value is 3,3 bar abs at 3°C boiling temperature measured
at the charging hose.
10 Evaporator:
(copper tube heat exchanger). The liquid refrigerant
evaporates inside the tube removing heat from the
temperature controlled water in the tank (boiling pressure 3°C, saturation pressure 3,3 bar abs).
11 Suction line:
(insulated copper tube). This transports the refrigerant
gas from the evaporator to the compressor.
12 High-pressure cut-off:
(pressure switch). This stops the refrigeration circuit if
the discharge pressure should exceed the maximum
allowed value due to a failure in the cooling water flow.
Set value for cut-off is 18 bar abs, diff. 4 bar.
