6.5 Meaning of bit positions when displaying the operating status
BYTE 1 = <B1>: ASCII characters 1 and 2
Bit 7 no meaning
Bit 6 = 1 –> Liquid level mark ”Full” exceeded
Bit 5 = 1 –> Liquid level mark ”ASM” not reached
Bit 4 = 1 –> Liquid level mark ”MIN” not reached (A)
Bit 3 = 1 –> Pump 2 (ext.) operating temperature
exceeded (A)
Bit 2 = 1 –> Pump 1 (int.) operating temperature
exceeded (A)
Bit 1 = 1 –> 27°C temperature exceeded (A)
Bit 0 = 1 –> 17°C temperature exceeded (A)
BYTE 2 = <B2>: ASCII characters 3 and 4
Bit 7 = 1 –> ALARM unit switches off automatically
Bit 6 = 1 –> Release START and STOP commands and
internal monitoring functions cannot be
carried out.
Bit 5 = 1 –> 3°C temperature not reached or (A)
40°C temperature exceeded
Bit 4 = 1 –> Temperature sensor breakage/ (A)
short-circuit
Bit 3 = 1 –> Calibration mistake (A)
Bit 2 = 1 –> Main contactor activated
( = 1 – at START
 = 0 – at STOP and ALARM)
Bit 1 = 1 –> Fault in heating circuit (A)
Bit 0 = 1 –> Operating pressure not reached (A)
BYTE 3 = <B3>: ASCII characters 5 and 6
Bit 7 = 1 –> Supply voltage not present in safety circuit
Bit 6 no meaning
Bit 5 no meaning
Bit 4 = 1 –> Hardware fault in main contactor (S)
Bit 3 = 1 –> Hardware fault in watchdog switching (S)
Bit 2 = 1 –> Hardware fault in alarm triggering (S)
switching
Bit 1 = 1 –> Hardware fault in unlocking switching (S)
Bit 0 = 1 –> Hardware fault in start test (S)
Character explanation
(A) – Alarm source
(S) – START and ER command cannot be carried out,
 unit must be switched off
